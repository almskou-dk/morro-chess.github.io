Chess is a game of decisions. MORRO is built around the tools that make those decisions feel better.

THE IDEA

Strategy deserves
a beautiful home.

MORRO is a concept store for players who care about the experience as much as the result. The collection balances classic materials, practical tournament equipment and statement pieces for the home.

From a first board to a collector's set, every product is presented with clear information and a consistent visual language.

Our philosophy is simple: choose well, set the board, and make your next move count.
